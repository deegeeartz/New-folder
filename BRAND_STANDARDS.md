## Brand Voice & Tone

### Core Voice Principles

1. **Clarity over jargon** — Explain technical concepts in business outcomes, not tech specs
2. **Confident but humble** — We know what we're doing; we don't oversell
3. **Action-oriented** — Focus on measurable results and next steps
4. **Global perspective** — Acknowledge different markets, regulations, and business contexts

### Tone Ratios

- **Professional (60%):** Credible, trustworthy, competent
- **Approachable (30%):** Conversational, responsive, collaborative
- **Ambitious (10%):** Vision-forward, growth-minded, innovative

## Visual & Typography Standards

### Color Usage

- **Primary Blue (#2563eb):** CTAs, headlines, brand moments
- **Cyan (#06b6d4):** Secondary accents, interactive elements
- **Magenta (#a855f7):** Gradients, hero section emphasis
- **Text (Dark mode):** #e2e8f0 (primary), #cbd5e1 (secondary)
- **Text (Light mode):** #0f172a (primary), #1e293b (secondary)

### Typography

- **Headlines:** Inter Bold/Extrabold, 150% line-height
- **Body copy:** Inter Regular, 1.6 line-height
- **CTAs:** Inter Bold, all-caps or title case (never sentence case)

### Animation & Micro-interactions

- **Hover states:** Subtle scale (1.05x) and shadow elevation
- **Transitions:** 250-300ms ease for UI feedback
- **Entrance animations:** Fade + slide-up over 700ms
- **Reduced motion:** Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion` setting

## CTA Patterns

### Primary CTA (Hero)

- **Button color:** Blue (#2563eb)
- **Text:** "Explore Services" or "Book a Strategy Call"
- **Context:** Always paired with secondary CTA offering alternative path

### Secondary CTA (Hero)

- **Button style:** Ghost (outline only)
- **Text:** "View Hardware Store" or "Learn More"
- **Context:** Lower commitment option

### Trust Section CTA

- **Inline text link** → "Schedule 20-min consultation"
- **Purpose:** Reduce friction by offering specific time commitment

### Exit / Engagement CTA

- **Full-width section** (CtaSection pattern)
- **Text:** "Transform your business with technology that scales."
- **Objection handling:** Include "No lock-in. Transparent pricing."

## Copy Editing Checklist

Before publishing any marketing copy, verify:

- [ ] **Outcome-focused?** Does it explain the business result, not the feature?
- [ ] **Global-aware?** Could this copy work for EU, Africa, and beyond?
- [ ] **Objection-addressed?** Do we handle "price", "timeline", "lock-in", "fit" concerns?
- [ ] **Action-oriented?** Does it include a clear next step (CTA)?
- [ ] **Contrasts with competitors?** Does it differentiate on transparency/experience?
- [ ] **Free of jargon?** Could a non-technical founder understand this?
- [ ] **Proof-backed?** Do we cite Lanzcape, ASMB, or specific metrics?
